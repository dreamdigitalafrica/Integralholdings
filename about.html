About Integral Holdings

Integral Holdings Ltd is a diversified Nigerian holding company established to build, develop and manage businesses across sectors fundamental to economic growth and long-term development.

About Integral Holdings

Incorporated in Nigeria on 30 August 2026 as a Private Company Limited by Shares, Integral Holdings operates as the strategic parent of a growing portfolio of specialized companies across construction, engineering, real estate, oil and gas, agriculture and automobiles.

The company is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC No. 9815739 and operates under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020.

At the heart of Integral Holdings is a clear purpose: building legacies and securing the future. The Group brings together businesses positioned within industries that directly influence how people live, work, move, produce and prosper.

One Group. Six Strategic Sectors. Building for Generations.
