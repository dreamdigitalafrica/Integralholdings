01 / Who We Are

About Integral Holdings

Integral Holdings Ltd is a diversified Nigerian holding company established to build, develop and manage businesses across sectors fundamental to economic growth and long-term development.

Incorporated in Nigeria on 30 August 2026 as a Private Company Limited by Shares, Integral Holdings operates as the strategic parent of a growing portfolio of specialized companies across construction, engineering, real estate, oil and gas, agriculture and automobiles. The company is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC No. 9815739 and operates under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020.

At the heart of Integral Holdings is a clear purpose: building legacies and securing the future. The Group brings together businesses positioned within industries that directly influence how people live, work, move, produce and prosper.

Through its subsidiaries, Integral Holdings seeks to participate meaningfully in infrastructure development, property and real estate, engineering solutions, energy, food and agricultural production, and mobility. Each subsidiary maintains a specialized sector focus while benefiting from the strategic direction, corporate identity and long-term investment philosophy of the wider Group.

Integral Holdings combines commercial ambition with a commitment to innovation, excellence, professionalism, transparency and people-oriented development. Its long-term ambition is to build businesses and assets that remain relevant beyond individual projects or transactions, creating sustainable value for clients, partners, employees, communities and future generations.

Leadership

Engr. Douglas Moshe

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Engr. Douglas Moshe is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Integral Holdings Ltd, providing strategic direction across the Group and its portfolio of operating companies.

His vision for Integral Holdings is centred on developing strong African enterprises capable of creating enduring commercial value while contributing meaningfully to infrastructure, industry, employment and economic development.

Under his leadership, the Group is establishing interests across construction, engineering, real estate, oil and gas, agriculture and automobiles, with each business united by a common philosophy of quality, integrity and long-term value creation.
